Fractions made friendly.

A fraction is just a way of showing part of a whole. Let's make sense of the pieces.

Meet the fraction

In ¾, the bottom number (the denominator) tells us the whole is split into 4 equal parts. The top number (the numerator) tells us we have 3 of them.

Imagine sharing a roti equally between four friends. Each friend gets ¼. Two friends together have 2/4, which is the same amount as ½.

Adding fractions with the same denominator

When the bottom numbers match, add only the top numbers. The size of each piece stays the same.

1/5 + 2/5=3/5One fifth plus two fifths makes three fifths.

When the bottom numbers are different

First, rewrite the fractions so they have the same denominator. For ½ + ¼, we can write ½ as 2/4. Now the pieces are the same size, so we can add them:

2/4 + 1/4=3/4Change one half into two quarters, then add.

Your turn

What is 2/6 + 3/6? Add the top numbers and keep the bottom number the same. You can simplify your answer if both numbers share a factor.

Answer: 5/6. Nice work!
