DIGITAL SKILLS · GETTING STARTED

How the web works.

Every time you open a website, a little conversation happens between your device and another computer.

What is the internet?

The internet is a worldwide network of connected computers and devices. They send information to one another over cables, Wi-Fi, and mobile networks.

What is a website?

A website is a collection of pages stored on a computer called a server. Each website has an address, like a street address, so your browser can find it.

What does a browser do?

A browser — such as Firefox, Chrome, or Safari — is the app you use to visit websites. When you enter a web address, your browser asks the website's server for its files. The server sends them back, and the browser puts them together on your screen.

Your browser↔Website serverThe browser asks for a page; the server sends the page back.

When you go offline

Without a connection, your device can't ask a server for new information. But files you've already saved on your device can still open — that's how LearnZA's offline study guides work.

Quick check

Which app helps you visit and view websites?

Answer: a web browser. It fetches pages and shows them to you.
